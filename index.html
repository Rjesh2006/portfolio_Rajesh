💡 About Me

I'm Rajesh, a Cloud Analyst at AW Infrasec Solutions Pvt. Ltd., where I administer Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive, Defender, Purview) and manage Azure infrastructure (VMs, VNets, NSGs) for enterprise clients.

I own Identity and Access Management through Microsoft Entra ID and administer Intune for device compliance, alongside managing Acronis Cyber Protect backups and recovery for client environments.

I led a full Google Workspace to Microsoft 365 migration and a device migration project moving clients from Hybrid Azure AD Join to Microsoft Entra Join using the Opsole Migration Tool. I also provide remote support for US-based clients via Zoho Desk, AnyDesk, and UltraViewer.

I hold a completed B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Science from Delhi University, and I'm currently working toward the AZ-104 (Microsoft Azure Administrator) certification.
